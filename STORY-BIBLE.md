# 💕 Strangers to Forever: Series Bible

A cute 2D animated Instagram reel series, told in short episodes (about 30 seconds each).
All names and places in this series are fictional stand-ins.

## 🎭 2. Character designs

Shared style: chibi proportions (big round head about 45% of height), dot-oval eyes with two sparkles, rosy blush, soft outlines, pastel palette.

### TARA (lead, narrator)
- **Vibe:** shy, kind, a little lost in the big city but brave. Small-town heart.
- **Look:** long dark-brown hair to mid-back, side-swept bangs, a tiny pink hair clip (her **signature prop**).
- **Outfit:** pink kurti-top `#FF8FAB` with a cream collar, lavender skirt, white sneakers. Carries a tote bag.
- **Expressions:** shy smile, worried brows, wide-eyed confusion (at the new phone), dreamy blush.
- **Arc:** didn't choose engineering, but engineering chose her person.

### KABIR (male lead)
- **Vibe:** calm, cheeky and confident. Quiet, but bold when it counts (he sends the request).
- **Look:** messy spiky black hair with one stubborn cowlick, thick brows, warmer skin tone.
- **Outfit:** sky-blue hoodie `#6EA8FE` over a white tee, navy jeans, backpack slung on one shoulder.
- **Signature:** earphones always around his neck.
- **Expressions:** smirk, surprised "oh!", soft smile at his phone.

### ZOYA (best friend, roommate)
- **Vibe:** loud, loyal, the funny sidekick. Hype-woman.
- **Look:** high round bun, round glasses, brown hair.
- **Outfit:** lavender top `#B69CFF`, denim overalls.
- **Signature:** always holding a snack or her phone.

### PIHU (classmate)
- **Vibe:** sweet, a bit chaotic, always on low battery.
- **Look:** two short pigtails, mint top `#9BE3C4`.
- **Signature:** a phone showing 1% battery and a "recharge" pack.

### APPA & AMMA (Tara's parents)
- **Appa:** moustache, orange shirt, strict but loving, arms folded.
- **Amma:** bun with a flower, green saree, worried-proud face.

## 🏙️ 3. Environment designs

| Location | Palette | Key elements |
|---|---|---|
| **Chandangaon** (hometown) | warm peach, leaf green, sunny yellow | tiled-roof houses, mango tree, a dusty lane, temple bell, sunset |
| **Tara's home** | cream walls, wood brown | window with curtains, family photo frame, sofa, the "big decision" table |
| **Train journey** | sunset orange to lilac | pink train, rolling hills, a city skyline rising in the distance |
| **Neelpur** (big city) | blue-violet, neon pink lights | tall towers, traffic, metro line, city lights at night |
| **Skyline Institute (SIT)** | sandy cream, brick red, campus green | big gate with sign, Lotus Block (pink trim) and Orchid Block (blue trim), the science lab, the canteen |
| **Girls' hostel room** | soft lavender | two beds, fairy lights, posters, suitcase |
| **Neelpur Central station** | grey-blue, yellow line | two platforms, two trains, departure board |

Color coding: **pink = Tara's world, blue = Kabir's world**. When they come together, scenes turn **purple**.

## 🎙️ 4. Voice casting

Generation note: use **Instagram's built-in text-to-speech** (free, in the reel editor), **ElevenLabs** (best quality), or record your own voice as narrator. Your own voice is the most authentic and performs best.

| Role | Voice brief | ElevenLabs-style pick |
|---|---|---|
| **Narrator (older Tara)** | warm, soft, smiling, Indian-English accent, slow storytelling pace | young female, warm, "storyteller", stability around 45% |
| **Kabir** | calm, low-mid, playful | young male, conversational, soft |
| **Zoya** | bright, fast, funny | young female, energetic |
| **Appa** | deep, firm, then gentle | middle-aged male, Indian accent |
| **Amma** | caring, slightly anxious | middle-aged female, Indian accent |

Music: soft lo-fi or acoustic romance at a low volume under the voice (pick trending audio in Instagram for reach).

## 🎬 6. Reel format (every episode)

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 30 seconds
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, cliffhanger in the last 3
- Captions kept inside the safe zone (away from Instagram's top bar and bottom buttons)
- Series badge "Strangers to Forever · Ep N" on every scene
- Caption template: `Ep N: <title> 💕 Part of our strangers-to-forever story. Follow for Ep N+1 ✨ #lovestory #animatedstory #collegelove #strangerstolovers #couplegoals`
