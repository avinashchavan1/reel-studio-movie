# Ep 1: Two Strangers (shot list, about 40 seconds, 8 shots × 5s)

For each shot: make the **image** (attach the character sheets, add the style block from STYLE-GUIDE.md),
then **animate** it with the motion prompt, then record the **voice** line.
Save as `ep01-shot1.mp4` … `ep01-shot8.mp4` and `ep01-vo1.mp3` … (or one full voice file).

---

### Shot 1: Hook
- **Image:** Ishita and Vihaan standing back-to-back on a busy Indian college campus road, both looking away from each other, not noticing, autos and students passing, golden-hour light.
- **Motion:** slow camera push-in, gentle breeze moves Ishita's dupatta and hair, students walk past in the background, both characters blink and glance sideways but never see each other.
- **Narrator:** "Two strangers… same city, same college… and no idea they were made for each other."

### Shot 2: Small-town girl
- **Image:** Ishita walking down a small Indian town lane (Dharwad) holding books, tiled-roof houses, a kirana shop, electric wires overhead, a cow resting by the road, morning sunlight.
- **Motion:** Ishita walks toward the camera with a soft smile, dupatta flowing, a shopkeeper waves at her, light camera tracking backward.
- **Narrator:** "This is Ishita. A simple girl from a small town called Dharwad."

### Shot 3: The decision
- **Image:** Inside a modest living room, Appa holding an engineering admission letter, Amma beside him smiling proudly, Ishita sitting on the sofa looking down, upset.
- **Motion:** Appa speaks firmly and taps the letter, Amma nods, Ishita's eyes fill with tears and she looks away, slow zoom on Ishita's face.
- **Appa:** "Engineering. Final."
- **Ishita (soft):** "But Appa… I never wanted engineering."

### Shot 4: Leaving home
- **Image:** Busy Indian railway platform (Dharwad station), Ishita with a suitcase hugging Amma, Appa holding her bag, train waiting.
- **Motion:** Ishita hugs Amma tightly, Appa pats her head, she boards the train and waves from the door as it starts moving.
- **Narrator:** "With one suitcase and a heavy heart… she left for the big city, Bangalore."

### Shot 5: Big city
- **Image:** Wide shot of a busy Indian metro city (Bangalore): flyover, glass towers, traffic, a yellow-green auto rickshaw. Ishita in the back seat of the auto looking out with wide amazed eyes.
- **Motion:** auto drives forward through traffic, Ishita looks around amazed, city lights and cars move past, slight camera shake.
- **Narrator:** "Everything was so big… so fast… so new."

### Shot 6: First day
- **Image:** Grand red-brick college gate with the sign "SILICON VALLEY INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY", cream colonial main building behind. Ishita and Zoya (round glasses, high bun) walking in together arm in arm, students around.
- **Motion:** both walk through the gate, Zoya chatting and laughing, Ishita smiles nervously, camera follows from behind then swings to their faces.
- **Zoya:** "Relax, roomie! It's just engineering… what's the worst that could happen?"

### Shot 7: Different blocks
- **Image:** Split composition. Left: Ishita in a classroom with "LOTUS BLOCK · SECTION 7" on the wall. Right: Vihaan in another classroom with "ORCHID BLOCK · SECTION 1", spinning a pen, earphones around his neck.
- **Motion:** Ishita writes notes and tucks her hair, Vihaan spins a pen and smirks at a friend, slow push-in on both.
- **Narrator:** "Same college. Different blocks. Total strangers."

### Shot 8: Cliffhanger
- **Image:** Night, Ishita in her hostel room sitting on the bed holding a brand-new smartphone in a gift box, looking confused, fairy lights on the wall.
- **Motion:** she turns the phone around, taps the screen, it lights up her face, a Wi-Fi symbol glows on the screen, camera slowly pushes in.
- **Narrator:** "But a tiny little thing… was about to change everything."
- **End text:** "Ep 2: The Smartphone 💕 Follow for next part"
